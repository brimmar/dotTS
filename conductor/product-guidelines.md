# Product Guidelines - dotts

## Voice and Tone
- **Helpful & Approachable:** The documentation and CLI should use friendly language to make the concept of "hermetic, type-safe dotfiles" feel accessible rather than intimidating.
- **Concise & Direct:** Respect the user's time by providing clear instructions and feedback without unnecessary fluff.
- **Empowering:** Frame features in terms of how they solve the user's "pain" and simplify their workflow.

## CLI Visual Style
- **Minimalist Aesthetics:** The CLI output should be clean and uncluttered, prioritizing readability over visual flair.
- **Subtle Highlighting:** Use color sparingly and purposefully (e.g., green for success, red for errors, yellow for warnings) to convey status without being distracting.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Provide high-level status by default, with flags available for more detailed or verbose output.

## Documentation Principles
- **Clarity First:** Use simple examples to demonstrate complex concepts.
- **Problem-Oriented:** Organize guides around specific tasks or common dotfile challenges.
- **Automated Verification:** Encourage users to lean on the type-safety features of the tool to verify their configurations.
